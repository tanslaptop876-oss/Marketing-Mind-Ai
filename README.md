# Marketing Mind AI

AI-powered marketing decision-support and content operations platform.

## Vision
Marketing Mind AI will help plan, create, organize, optimize, and measure digital marketing across websites and social channels from one workflow.

## MVP roadmap
- Brand and audience profiles
- Content idea and campaign planning engine
- Multi-channel content calendar
- AI content brief/copy generation layer
- SEO workflow and keyword organization
- Social publishing workflow adapters
- Campaign analytics and performance scoring
- Dashboard and recommendations

## Development approach
Work is developed in small tested branches and merged through pull requests.

## Status
Foundation stage.
